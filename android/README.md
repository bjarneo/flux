# Flux for Android

Read the [Android guide](../docs/android.md) for requirements, APK installation, local builds, tests, and screenshots.
Read the [release guide](../docs/releasing.md) for APK signatures and GitHub Actions.

The app opens on the **Inbox**: 1 list of what happens on all paired computers. What needs you comes first, in the large tile at the top.
The navigation bar holds the **Inbox**, **Send**, **Control**, and **Computers**. A wide window, such as a phone in landscape or a tablet, shows them in a navigation rail at the side.
Finished transfers, the last clip, and a paused player leave the Inbox after 30 minutes.
The scope chip at the top selects all computers or 1 computer.
The page names for `tools/shot.sh` are in the Test section of the [Android guide](../docs/android.md#test).
